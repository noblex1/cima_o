ARBITRATION LOGOS - Download Instructions
==========================================

Please download the official logos from these websites and save them in this folder:

1. icc-logo.png - https://iccwbo.org/ (look for their logo in footer or about page)
2. lcia-logo.png - https://www.lcia.org/ 
3. siac-logo.png - https://siac.org.sg/
4. hkiac-logo.png - https://www.hkiac.org/
5. scc-logo.png - https://sccarbitrationinstitute.se/
6. icdr-logo.png - https://www.icdr.org/

Logo Requirements:
- Format: PNG with transparent background (preferred) or JPG
- Size: Approximately 200-300px wide
- High resolution for retina displays

Once downloaded, the website will automatically display them!
