# Career Opportunities - User Guide

## Quick Access

### How to View Opportunities
There are **three ways** to access the career opportunities:

1. **From the Homepage**
   - Scroll down to the **"Join Our Global Team"** section (burgundy background)
   - Click the **"View Opportunities"** button to see full details
   - Or click **"Apply Now"** to send an email directly

2. **From the Footer**
   - Scroll to the bottom of any page
   - Look for **"About CIMA"** section in the footer
   - Click on **"Careers"** link

3. **Direct URL**
   - Navigate to: `https://thecima.org/opportunities`

## How to Apply

### Application Methods

#### Option 1: Direct Email (Recommended)
Click **"Apply Now"** button on the opportunities page, which will:
- Open your default email client
- Pre-fill the recipient: info@thecima.org
- Pre-fill the subject: "Business Development Volunteer Application"
- You just need to:
  1. Attach your CV/Resume
  2. Write your cover letter in the email body
  3. Click Send

#### Option 2: Manual Email
Send an email to: **info@thecima.org**

**Subject Line:** Business Development Volunteer Application

**Include:**
- Your full name
- Location/Country
- CV/Resume (attached)
- Cover letter explaining:
  - Your interest in the role
  - Relevant experience
  - Availability
  - Why you want to join CIMA

## FAQ

### Q: Is this a paid position?
**A:** The initial 3-month engagement is a volunteer position. However, high-performing volunteers may be considered for paid, remote, part-time employment upon successful completion.

### Q: What is the time commitment?
**A:** 10-15 hours per week for the three-month period.

### Q: Do I need a legal background?
**A:** No, a legal background is not required. We're looking for individuals interested in business development and international partnerships.

### Q: Is this position fully remote?
**A:** Yes, the position is primarily remote with flexible hours.

### Q: When does the position start?
**A:** We have rolling admissions, so you can apply at any time.

### Q: How many positions are available?
**A:** We are seeking 6 Business Development Volunteers worldwide.

### Q: What happens after I apply?
**A:** You'll receive an initial review, and selected candidates will be invited for a virtual interview with our leadership team.

### Q: Can I apply from any country?
**A:** Yes! This is a global opportunity open to candidates worldwide.

## Tips for Applicants

### ✨ Best Practices

**Before Applying:**
- ✓ Read the full opportunity description carefully
- ✓ Review CIMA's mission and values on the About page
- ✓ Prepare a tailored CV highlighting relevant experience
- ✓ Draft a compelling cover letter

**In Your Application:**
- ✓ Explain why you're interested in CIMA specifically
- ✓ Highlight any international experience
- ✓ Mention languages you speak
- ✓ Demonstrate your self-motivation and remote work ability
- ✓ Clearly state your availability (10-15 hours/week)

**Email Etiquette:**
- ✓ Use a professional email address
- ✓ Write a clear, concise subject line
- ✓ Attach documents in PDF format
- ✓ Name files clearly: "YourName_CV.pdf", "YourName_CoverLetter.pdf"
- ✓ Proofread before sending
