## What is CIMA?

The **Center for International Mediators and Arbitrators (CIMA)** is a leading organization headquartered in Oxford and Accra, dedicated to:

- **Training and certifying** lawyers and ADR professionals
- **Providing dispute resolution services** through arbitration and mediation
- **Building a global network** of qualified neutrals across 33+ countries
- **Offering professional recognition** through internationally benchmarked credentials

## Main Services Offered

### **1. Training & Certification Programs**

CIMA offers comprehensive training for ADR professionals at three levels:

#### **Associate Level (ACIMArb)**
- **Who It's For**: Early-career professionals, students, and those new to ADR
- **What You Get**: Foundation training in arbitration and mediation principles
- **Requirements**: Bachelor's degree or professional qualification; completion of foundation course
- **Ideal For**: Building your knowledge base in dispute resolution

#### **Member Level (MCIMArb)**
- **Who It's For**: Experienced professionals ready to practice as arbitrators/mediators
- **What You Get**: Advanced practitioner training with hands-on simulations
- **Requirements**: 3-5 years professional experience; advanced course completion; professional references
- **Ideal For**: Establishing yourself as a qualified neutral

#### **Fellowship Level (FCIMArb)**
- **Who It's For**: Senior practitioners with distinguished careers
- **What You Get**: Recognition as a leading expert in ADR
- **Requirements**: 10+ years experience; demonstrated case experience; significant contributions to the field
- **Ideal For**: Senior lawyers and ADR leaders seeking top-tier recognition

**How to Apply:**
Visit the **Courses** page to see upcoming programs, then click "REGISTER" to complete your application online.

### **4. Dispute Resolution Services**

If you have a dispute to resolve, CIMA provides professional arbitration and mediation services.

#### **How to Submit a Dispute**

**Step 1: Review Your Case**
- Ensure you have an arbitration agreement or clause
- Gather contract documents, correspondence, and evidence
- Identify the parties involved and relief sought

**Step 2: Complete the Request Form**
- Download the Request for Arbitration/Mediation form
- Provide comprehensive details about the dispute
- Specify your preferred neutrals (optional)

**Step 3: Submit with Filing Fee**
- Filing fees range from $500 to $25,000 based on claim amount
- Contact the Registry for specific fee calculations

**Step 4: Case Registration**
- CIMA's Registry reviews your submission
- You receive a case reference number within 3 business days
- A case manager is assigned to your dispute

**Step 5: Tribunal Constitution**
- Select arbitrators/mediators from CIMA's Roll
- CIMA assists with tribunal formation
- Proceedings commence according to CIMA Rules

**Where to Start**: Click **Contact Us** → **Submit a Dispute**

## Registration & Application Processes

### **To Register for Courses:**

1. Navigate to **Courses** page
2. Review available programs and dates
3. Click **REGISTER** button on your selected course
4. Complete the online form with:
   - Full name and contact information
   - Professional background
   - Organization details (if applicable)
   - Payment information
5. Submit and receive confirmation email

**Payment Options:**
- Individual registration: Pay directly through online portal
- Team/Organization registration: Contact info@thecima.org for special arrangements

### **To Apply for Membership:**

1. Navigate to **Membership** → **CIMA Qualification Pathway**
2. Choose your membership level:
   - Associate (ACIMArb)
   - Member (MCIMArb)
   - Fellow (FCIMArb)
3. Review requirements for your chosen level
4. Download application form
5. Prepare supporting documents:
   - Professional CV
   - Academic certificates
   - Training certifications
   - Professional references (2-3)
   - Recent photograph
6. Submit application with fee:
   - Associate: $200
   - Member: $400
   - Fellow: $600
7. Wait 4-6 weeks for review
8. Receive membership certificate and designation

### **To Register as an Arbitrator/Mediator:**

1. Navigate to **Contact Us** → **Register as an Arbitrator**
2. Click **CLICK HERE TO REGISTER NOW** button
3. Complete the online registration form
4. Upload required documents
5. Submit and await confirmation

## Membership Renewals

All CIMA members must renew annually to maintain their standing.

### **Renewal Requirements:**

**Associate Members:**
- Annual fee: Varies by region
- CPD requirement: 10 hours per year

**Members:**
- Annual fee: Varies by region
- CPD requirement: 15 hours per year

**Fellows:**
- Annual fee: Varies by region
- CPD requirement: 20 hours per year

### **How to Renew:**

1. Navigate to **Membership** → **Membership Renewals**
2. Review renewal deadlines and requirements
3. Submit CPD record
4. Pay renewal fee online
5. Receive renewed certificate

**Important**: Failure to renew results in lapsed membership and removal from the Roll.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does certification take?**
A: Foundation courses range from 5-10 days. Advanced programs can take 2-4 weeks. Full certification (Associate to Fellow) can take 3-10 years depending on your experience.

**Q: Are CIMA credentials recognized internationally?**
A: Yes. CIMA credentials are recognized across 33+ countries and meet international standards.

**Q: Can my law firm become a CIMA institutional member?**
A: Yes. Contact membership@thecima.org for institutional membership applications.

**Q: How much does it cost to submit a dispute?**
A: Filing fees range from $500 to $25,000 based on claim amount. Arbitrator fees are separate and calculated per case.

**Q: Can I take courses online?**
A: Yes. CIMA offers both online and in-person training programs.

**Q: How do I find an arbitrator for my case?**
A: Visit **Membership** → **Roll of Mediators & Arbitrators** to browse qualified neutrals by practice area.

**Q: What if my membership lapses?**
A: You can apply for reinstatement, but may need to complete additional CPD hours and pay reinstatement fees.
