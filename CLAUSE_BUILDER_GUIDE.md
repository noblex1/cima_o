# CIMA Clause Builder - User Guide

## Overview

The CIMA Clause Builder is an interactive web tool that helps individuals, businesses, and organizations create clear, professionally structured arbitration and mediation clauses for their contracts.

## Features

### 1. **Multi-Step Wizard Interface**
- Intuitive step-by-step process
- Progress tracking with visual indicators
- Easy navigation between steps

### 2. **Clause Types Available**
- **Arbitration Clause**: Traditional binding arbitration
- **Mediation Clause**: Facilitated negotiation and settlement
- **Med-Arb Clause**: Mediation first, then arbitration if needed
- **Arb-Med Clause**: Arbitration with mediation during the process
- **Escalation Clause**: Multi-tiered (negotiation → mediation → arbitration)

### 3. **30+ Agreement Types Supported**
Including but not limited to:
- Commercial agreements (Supply, Distribution, Service)
- Employment and Consultancy agreements
- Technology agreements (Software, SaaS, AI Services)
- Construction agreements
- Partnership and Joint Venture agreements
- And many more...

### 4. **Customization Options**

#### Basic Settings
- **Jurisdiction/Seat**: Choose from 30+ jurisdictions worldwide
- **Language**: 14 major languages supported
- **Number of Arbitrators**: 1, 3, or custom number

#### Advanced Options
- Emergency arbitrator provisions
- Expedited procedures
- Confidentiality clauses
- Binding settlement agreements
- Custom time limits for mediation/arbitration
- Same neutral for Med-Arb proceedings

### 5. **Output Features**
- Preview generated clause
- Copy to clipboard
- Download as text file
- Professional formatting

## How to Use

### Step 1: Accept Terms & Conditions
1. Read the disclaimer carefully
2. Check the acceptance box
3. Click "Continue"

**Important Disclaimer Points:**
- Tool is for educational and informational purposes only
- Does not create lawyer-client relationship
- Legal advice should be obtained before use
- CIMA does not guarantee suitability or enforceability
- Usage does not mean CIMA will administer disputes

### Step 2: Choose Clause Type
1. Review the 5 available clause types
2. Click on your preferred type
3. The selected card will highlight
4. Click "Continue"

**Clause Type Descriptions:**
- **Arbitration**: Direct to binding arbitration
- **Mediation**: Attempt settlement through mediation
- **Med-Arb**: Try mediation first, arbitration if unsuccessful
- **Arb-Med**: Arbitration with mediation opportunity
- **Escalation**: Progressive resolution (negotiate → mediate → arbitrate)

### Step 3: Select Agreement Type
1. Open the dropdown menu
2. Choose your agreement type from 30+ options
3. Confirmation message appears
4. Click "Continue"

### Step 4: Customize Your Clause
1. **Required Fields:**
   - Seat/Place of Arbitration
   - Language

2. **Arbitration-Specific Options:**
   - Number of arbitrators
   - Arbitration rules (CIMA, ICC, LCIA, UNCITRAL, etc.)
   - Emergency arbitrator provision
   - Expedited procedure

3. **Mediation-Specific Options:**
   - Mediation rules
   - Time limits
   - Binding settlement provision

4. **Optional Features:**
   - Check/uncheck as needed
   - Hover over options for more information

5. Click "Continue" when done

### Step 5: Review & Generate
1. Review your selections in the summary
2. Click "Generate Clause"
3. Your custom clause appears
4. **Actions Available:**
   - **Copy**: Copy clause to clipboard
   - **Download**: Save as text file

## Best Practices

### 1. **Preparation**
- Understand your dispute resolution needs
- Know your jurisdiction requirements
- Consider the nature of your agreement

### 2. **Customization**
- Choose appropriate number of arbitrators (1 for speed/cost, 3 for complex disputes)
- Select jurisdiction familiar to both parties
- Use language all parties understand
- Consider emergency arbitrator for urgent matters

### 3. **Review**
- Always review generated clauses with legal counsel
- Ensure enforceability in your jurisdiction
- Verify compliance with local laws
- Adapt language to your specific needs

### 4. **Integration**
- Place clause in appropriate section of contract
- Ensure consistency with other contract terms
- Reference governing law provisions
- Update existing contracts if needed

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot proceed to next step:**
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Check that terms and conditions are accepted
- Verify a clause type is selected

**Generated clause looks incorrect:**
- Review your selections in Step 5
- Use Back button to modify choices
- Regenerate clause after changes

**Copy/Download not working:**
- Ensure clause is generated first
- Check browser permissions
- Try alternative browser if issues persist
